01 — Introduction
Platform Overview
What CareerPath Africa is, who it serves, and how it works.

CareerPath Africa is a comprehensive e-learning and career development platform built for African learners and educators. It delivers structured programmes, live instruction, professional mentorship, and internationally verifiable certificates — all in one place, accessible from any device.

Structured Courses

Self-paced video modules you can watch anytime — individually or as part of a package bundle.

Live Classes

Scheduled interactive sessions on Daily.co — attend live or watch recordings later.

Mentorship

Book private 1-on-1 video sessions with your own teachers. Teachers open time slots; students browse, pick a slot, add a note on what to discuss, and confirm — the session room is auto-created.

Certificates

Earn verifiable PDF certificates shareable on LinkedIn or with any employer.

Integrated Wallet

Token-based wallet for course access, in-platform spending, and affiliate payouts.

Pan-African Reach

Country-specific pricing and packages managed through local Country Representatives.

Search & Discovery

Instant full-text search across all published courses and teacher profiles with results in real time.

Direct Messaging

Private iMessage-style inbox for students and teachers — start a conversation from any teacher profile.

Reputation Badges

Teacher reputation badges (Top Rated, Rising Star) computed automatically from verified student reviews.

Multilingual (EN / FR)

Switch the interface language between English and French at any time via the Language Switcher in the topbar.

Quizzes & Assessments

MCQ, True/False, Fill-in-the-blank, and written quizzes with auto-grading, time limits, and per-question analytics.

Gamification & XP

Earn XP for every action, level up, maintain daily streaks, unlock badges, and compete on the cohort leaderboard.

Flashcards

Spaced-repetition flashcard decks created by teachers or AI-generated from lesson content — Easy/Hard/Again rating system.

AI Study Assistant

Chat with an AI tutor grounded in your lesson content — also generates quiz questions and provides writing feedback.

Three Ways to Learn on CareerPath Africa

Students can access content through any of these three independent learning paths — they are not mutually exclusive.

1 — Self-Paced Course

Buy a single course and work through video modules at your own pace. No schedule, no teacher interaction required. Best for learners who want to pick up a specific skill on their own timeline.

2 — Package (Bundle)

Buy a package (e.g. "ACCA Foundation Bundle") to unlock access to multiple courses at once. Still self-paced — just more content in one purchase. Packages are country-priced and managed by Country Representatives.

3 — Cohort (Teacher-Led)

Placed by admin into a structured exam-prep group with a dedicated teacher and a fixed exam date. Includes live classes, assignments, grading, attendance tracking, and an exam countdown. The premium track for professional exam sittings (WASSCE, ACCA, Cambridge, etc.).

They are independent. A student can have a self-paced course, an active package, and be enrolled in a cohort — all at the same time. Cohorts are not required; they are only applicable to students doing structured teacher-led exam prep.

WordPress Integration — Built on Your Existing Infrastructure

CareerPath Africa does not replace your existing WordPress website. The new portal is built on top of it. Your WordPress site remains the primary content catalogue and is the source of record for course descriptions, media, and pricing. The portal pulls this content in automatically and adds the management, student, and teacher layers on top.

What this means for you: Content your team creates on WordPress is immediately available in the portal without re-entering it. Your existing SEO, domain authority, and content library are fully preserved.
ConcernAnswer
Do I lose my WP site? No. WordPress continues to run exactly as it does today. The portal is an addition, not a replacement.
How are WP courses imported? Admin clicks Sync WordPress Courses on the Courses page. The system calls POST /platform-courses/wp-sync and pulls in new and updated courses automatically. Each course retains its WP Post ID and a direct link back to the original WordPress page.
What is the "WP Reviews" page? Admin assigns each imported WP course to a teacher (who will teach it) and optionally a Subject Reviewer (who checks quality). Both receive a commission percentage. Reviewers approve or reject with written notes before the course goes live.
Can I still edit courses on WP? Yes. WP courses are managed on the WordPress site. The portal shows an Open in WordPress button next to every WP-sourced course so admins can jump directly to the WP editor.
What are "Native" courses? Courses created directly inside the portal by teachers using the built-in course builder (video modules, rich text, quizzes). These live entirely in the CPA database and are independent of WordPress.

03 — Onboarding
First-Time Setup
Five steps to complete before you start learning.
  • Find your Student ID — Check your welcome email for your unique Student ID (e.g. SN-A3F2B891). Keep it handy — you will need it for support requests. It is always visible in Settings → Profile as well.
  • Complete your profile — Go to Settings → Profile. Upload a profile photo, select your country, add a phone number, and write a short bio. A complete profile helps teachers and mentors connect with you.
  • Browse the Catalogue — Visit the Catalogue page to explore all published programmes. Filter by category, level, or search by keyword.
  • Select a Package — Packages bundle one or more courses at a fixed price. Your Country Representative may offer country-specific pricing. Go to Billing to see all available packages.
  • Complete payment — The payment method depends on the package currency: Paystack (cards, bank transfer, USSD — NGN, GHS, ZAR, KES, EGP), Tranzak (MTN MoMo & Orange Money — XAF, XOF and other Central/West African currencies), or Stripe (international cards — USD, EUR, GBP and more). You are redirected back to the portal automatically after a successful payment.
  • Install the app (optional) — On mobile, tap your browser menu and choose Add to Home Screen to install CareerPath Africa as a PWA for offline access and push notifications.
Already have a careerpathafrica.com account? Sign in with the same email and password — no need to register again. On your first sign-in you’ll be asked for your country (and optionally your phone number) before choosing your programmes.
Pro tip: Allow browser notifications when prompted. You will receive real-time alerts when a live class is about to start, when an assignment is graded, and when your teacher posts a cohort announcement.

Mentorship — How It Works

Mentorship is the 1-on-1 private video session system. The mentors are your teachers — there is no separate mentor role. Any teacher who opens availability slots appears in the student Browse Mentors list.

Teacher opens slots

Teacher goes to Mentorship → My Slots, picks a date/time, sets duration (15, 30, 45, or 60 min), and saves. The slot is immediately visible to students.

Student books a slot

Student browses mentors, selects a teacher, picks an available slot, writes a note on what to discuss, then confirms. Booking status becomes Confirmed.

Session & review

A private Daily.co video room is auto-created. Both sides click Join up to 15 min before start. After the session the student can leave a star rating and comment.

As a Student — step by step
1
Go to Mentorship → Browse Mentors
Each teacher card shows their subjects, average star rating, review count, and the number of open slots. Click any card to load their available times on the right.
2
Pick a slot and add a note
Choose a date/time that suits you. In the confirmation modal, write what you want to cover — e.g. "Help with ACCA F3 Chapter 4 — I'm struggling with consolidation". The teacher sees this before the session so they can prepare.

Confirm and wait for session time
Your booking appears in My Sessions. The Join button activates 15 minutes before the scheduled start. It pulses blue when the session is live.

Leave a review
After the session ends, a ★ Review button appears on your booking card. Rate 1–5 stars and add an optional comment. Reviews are shown publicly on the teacher's profile and affect their reputation badge.
Cancellation policy: You can cancel a confirmed booking any time before the session. An optional reason field is provided. The teacher is notified by email immediately and the slot becomes available for other students. You will also receive a cancellation confirmation email.
